# Organiser reply to the team's questions, 7 November 2026

Source: reply from the challenge organisers to questions the team sent, received 2026-11-07, pasted by Arda. Verbatim below; our reading of it is in `plans/2026-11-07-release-gate.md` and the README, not here.

---

Hi! Thank you for the thoughtful questions and for taking the time to understand the challenge so thoroughly.

Your understanding is aligned with the challenge objectives. We'd also like to clarify a few points for everyone working on TrustED.

**1. Privacy technology vs. practical value**

Both are important. The challenge is about applying appropriate Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs) to real data-sharing problems while preserving the usefulness of the data.

The key is to demonstrate what is being protected, why the selected methods are appropriate, how useful the data remains, and how you measure the results.

**2. One use case or multiple?**

There is no requirement or automatic advantage to covering multiple scenarios.

Both approaches are valid. Solutions are evaluated based on the published criteria: technical implementation, innovation, impact, and presentation, rather than simply the number of scenarios supported.

Focus on demonstrating the quality and effectiveness of your solution.

**3. Conflicting privacy and utility requirements**

Identifying and explaining these trade-offs is an important part of the challenge.

If privacy and utility objectives conflict, you can explain your decisions and demonstrate their impact through measurements. Be transparent about which targets are achieved, which are not, and what limitations remain.

The goal is not to claim perfect protection, but to demonstrate a well-reasoned and measurable approach.
