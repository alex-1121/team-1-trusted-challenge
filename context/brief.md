# TrustED PETs Transformer

Challenge brief · Since AI Hackathon 2026 · 6–8 Nov 2026, Turku

Build a working privacy-enhancing tool that protects synthetic data while keeping it useful for analysis. Identify risks, choose proportionate transformations and demonstrate the trade-off. Use only synthetic, public or expressly approved data.

Inspired by TrustED · EU Horizon Europe grant agreement No. 101168467 · Challenge hosted by Since AI

Challenge partner (project)

TrustED · Horizon Europe project (Grant Agreement 101168467) Coordinator: Gradiant · Co-funding partner: TREE

## 01 Challenge Title

A clear, memorable name for the one working prototype you will build.

TrustED PETs Transformer Protect the data, keep its value.

## 02 Problem Statement

Why do data-sharing teams need to protect sensitive fields without destroying useful information?

Organisations need to share and analyse valuable datasets without exposing personal, sensitive or restricted information. Removing names alone rarely prevents re-identification; excessive protection can destroy useful patterns, trends and relationships. Build a practical way to balance both.

## 03 Guiding Question

What must your prototype detect, transform and demonstrate by Sunday?

How might we automatically identify privacy risks in a synthetic dataset, select suitable privacy-enhancing transformations and demonstrate that the protected result still supports a defined analytical purpose?

## 04 Recommended AI Approaches (Optional)

Choose only privacy techniques suited to the data, threat model and intended use case.

Possible PETs include masking, pseudonymisation, tokenisation, generalisation, binning, suppression, aggregation, synthetic-data generation, text redaction, rounding of time/location, and differential privacy for aggregate queries. Use AI only where it adds value; justify every technique and disclose residual risks.

## 05 Dataset or API Provided (If any)

Use safe test data. A separate synthetic example may be made available; no private dataset or API is required.

No real personal, customer or confidential consortium data is required or permitted without explicit authorisation.

Use realistic, fully synthetic sample data (or your own synthetic/public dataset) featuring identifiers, quasi-identifiers, dates, locations, free text and measurements. A separate starter dataset may be published in the participant materials. No production data, confidential inputs, mandatory API or TrustED account is required.

## 06 Evaluation Criteria

Show both improved privacy protection and measurable data usefulness; explain any limitations.

Judging uses Since AI’s existing criteria: (1) Technical implementation — correct, reliable PET choices; (2) Innovation — intelligent, explainable approach; (3) Impact — evidence of reduced disclosure risk with preserved analytical utility; (4) Presentation — clear scenario, before/after comparison and honest limitations. No new weighted rubric is imposed.

## 07 Submission Format

Submit through sinceai.app, following the event-wide submission rules and deadlines.

Submit one working prototype in sinceai.app with a short demo video (English narration recommended), a clear description, and the presentation / repository materials required by the platform. Show the identified data types, PET choices, protected output, measurable before/after utility and one realistic sharing scenario. Never include real sensitive data or secrets in public materials.
