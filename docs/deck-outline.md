# Pitch deck outline

One line per slide: title - what it says - the one number or picture it carries (source file). Scorecard numbers are our self-assessment from the organisers' `pets-eval` and are separate from the official judging score.

1. **TrustED PETs Transformer** - one tool: detect, select, protect, repair, attack, then release or refuse - picture: the one build command (`README.md`, "How to run").
2. **The problem** - raw emergency-department (ED) data re-identifies patients, and blanking it kills the research - number: raw linkage re-identification 0.5697 (`baseline-scores/health_HEALTH-S1_research-env_raw.json`).
3. **The scenario: HEALTH-S1** - research group in a research environment, five analyses must still work - picture: recipient, purpose and the five tasks (`contracts/HEALTH-S1.json`). Demo item 5.
4. **The official score was wrong** - our earlier release scored composite 0.9709 with linkage 0.0; our red team rebuilt every dropped date (1.0) and named 10 news-clip patients, all correctly (`results/redteam/before-7cf541b.json`). Picture: visit ids in order, the hour going back, a new day.
5. **Detect: types found from values alone** - health held-out macro-F1 0.8202 against the B1 target 0.65, not a clean held-out score (`results/detect/health.json`, `results/detect/RESULTS.md`, `results/detect/SPLIT.md`); 26 unseen columns labelled (`results/detect/unseen_field_labels.csv`). Demo item 1.
6. **Select: the tool picks the PET** - every candidate tried and measured, the most useful one kept under a stated safety line (risk 0.05 on the columns an outsider could know), a column kept only if it improves a task by at least 0.01 over dropping it (skipped where dropping breaks a task); ed_visits ends above the line after repair (0.1547, triage counted as known) and the reasons of the knowable columns and of the columns judged after triage say "line not met"; on HEALTH-S1 the line changed no choice (it did real work on arrival_ts only, where date and hour was feasible at risk 0.999 and hour of day 0.0227 is under the line, and still landed on v1's pick); arrival time: month and week give H-U8 0.0, hour of day 1.0; sex and most vitals dropped because no task needs them (`contracts/HEALTH-S1.json`, `results/selection/HEALTH-S1.v2.json`). Demo item 2.
7. **Repair only the rows at risk** - 3746 rows (0.0807) coarsened step by step until k of at least 5 and 2 distinct diagnoses, including among visits with labs, each coarsened value written as a range; 6 step orders scored (`results/health-s1-build/repair_report.json`, `results/health-s1-build/repair_search.md`).
8. **The red-team gate** - five attacks the scorecard does not run; the build refuses to write `pets_submission/` when one succeeds, a task falls under 0.5, a group falls under k 5 or l 2, or a stray file appears. It refuses to run at all on an unsigned or changed policy: the hospital's data steward signs the risk limits, the tool never sets them (`policy/HEALTH-S1.policy.json`, `release_record.json`) - numbers: dates rebuilt 1.0 to 0.0002, patients named 10 to 0, fingerprint 0.3544 to not applicable; the organisers' suggested patient key fails at 0.2935 (`results/redteam/before-7cf541b.json`, `results/health-s1-build/redteam.json`, `results/health-s1-pseudonym-build/redteam.json`). Demo item 3.
9. **Before and after** - privacy 0.1716 to 1.0, utility 1.0 to 0.971, composite 0.2752 to 0.9866; H-U8 0.9446 is the largest price (`baseline-scores/health_HEALTH-S1_research-env_raw.json`, `results/health-s1-build/scorecard.json`); organiser B2-k utility 0.40 for contrast (`data/proving-ground/docs/BENCHMARK.md`). Demo item 4.
10. **Same tool, other recipients** - no hand-written rules: HEALTH-S3 AI vendor 0.2769 to 1.0, EDU-S2 edtech vendor 0.3395 to 0.9983, ENERGY-S3 energy-poverty research 0.3506 to 1.0, PUB-S3 refused: a reviewer's duration column rescues its research question (0.9767), but the hotspot map and area counts cannot survive k of at least 5 in one row-level table (`results/health-s3-build/`, `results/edu-s2-build/`, `results/pub-s3-build/redteam.json`).
11. **Honest limits** - the signature shows which policy the files were built under; nobody independent has checked our protection, and nothing we ship controls the data once the university has it; an attacker who already knows a visit's diagnosis group, age band, municipality and hour singles out 0.0971 of visits (`results/health-s1-build/checks/informed_attacker.json`), and one contract line closes it at composite 0.9647 (`results/health-s1-informed-build/`); no dates, no seasonality; for a trusted research environment, not public release (`docs/guarantee-and-residual-risk.md`). Footer: "TrustED PETs Proving Ground 2026, synthetic data by Since AI ry (CC BY 4.0)".
12. **Where this goes** - a release gate for any data leaving a company: contract as code, select, attack, repair, receipt. Sold to the hospital's data steward or data protection officer, who stays accountable; it runs where the data sits and produces signed evidence for their decision. Vision slide, no numbers.
